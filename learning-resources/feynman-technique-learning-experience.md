# 🧠 Learning with the Feynman Technique

## My Experience Learning Machine Learning with Robotics

### 1. The Challenge

While studying **Machine Learning with Robotics Modules**, I faced a challenge.

Some lessons were difficult to understand because they contained complex concepts, technical explanations, and unfamiliar terminology.

I realized that simply reading the lessons several times wasn't helping me understand the topics deeply.

I needed a different approach to learning.

### 2. Discovering a Better Learning Method

I decided to experiment with the **Feynman Technique**, a learning method based on a simple idea:

> If you can't explain something in simple terms, you probably don't understand it well enough.

The main idea is to break down complex concepts into simpler explanations that anyone can understand.

### 3. How I Used NotebookLM

To apply this method, I uploaded my lesson files to **NotebookLM** and asked it to help me understand the material step by step.

My instructions were:

- Explain the lessons in simple language using the Feynman Technique.
- Break down complex topics into smaller, understandable steps.
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.
- Use simple examples and analogies.
- Explain where and how each concept is used in the real world.
- Show practical applications, especially in robotics and machine learning.

This helped me approach difficult topics from a different perspective.

### 4. What Made This Approach Helpful?

Instead of just reading complicated technical explanations, I could explore each concept through simpler descriptions and practical examples.

For example, when learning a machine learning concept, understanding its definition was not enough.

I also wanted to know:

- What does this concept actually mean?
- Why do we need it?
- How does it work?
- Where can we see it in real-world applications?
- How can it be used in robotics?

Connecting theoretical knowledge to real-world applications made the lessons easier to understand and remember.

### 5. What I Learned from This Experience

This experience taught me that learning is not just about collecting information.

It's about building a clear understanding of what we study.

I also realized that AI tools such as NotebookLM can be useful learning assistants when we give them clear instructions and actively evaluate their explanations.

However, reading simplified explanations is only the beginning. To make sure I truly understand a topic, I still need to explain it in my own words, solve problems, and apply it in practice.

### 6. My Takeaway

The Feynman Technique changed the way I approached difficult learning materials.

Whenever I face a complicated topic, I try to:

1. Break it into smaller concepts.
2. Understand each concept using simple explanations.
3. Connect it to real-world examples.
4. Explain it in my own words.
5. Identify gaps in my understanding and revisit them.

**My biggest lesson:**

> Don't just try to memorize complicated concepts. Try to understand them well enough to explain them simply.

### 🛠️ Tools I Used

- **Feynman Technique:** To approach complex concepts through simple explanations and deeper understanding.

### 🌱 Final Thought

Learning becomes easier when we stop asking only *"What should I memorize?"* and start asking *"How can I truly understand this?"*

**Learn Simply. Think Deeply. Explain Clearly.**
